Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to enes3rkan@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS version. You can also send feedback from inside the game: Profile → Settings → Send feedback.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
On your old device open Settings → Move to another device to get a 6-letter code. On the new device tap "I have a code" on the start screen or in Settings.
Will I lose my progress?
Not if cloud save is on (it is by default). Your progress is backed up automatically with a private random ID.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete my cloud data removes your cloud save and every report sent from your device. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. You can also e-mail us and we will delete it for you.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. TileKingdom plays fully offline. A connection is only used for cloud backup.
The game feels slow on my device.
Turn off High quality in Settings.
There is no sound.
Check the silent switch on your iPhone and the Music and Sound effects toggles in Settings.

Destek (Türkçe)

Yardım, hata bildirimi veya öneri için enes3rkan@icloud.com adresine cihaz modelini ve iOS sürümünü yazarak ulaşabilirsin. Oyun içinden de yazabilirsin: Profil → Ayarlar → Geri bildirim gönder.

Yeni telefona nasıl geçerim?
Eski cihazda Ayarlar → Başka cihaza taşı ile 6 harfli kodu al; yeni cihazda açılışta ya da Ayarlar'da "Kodum var"a gir.
Verimi nasıl silerim?
Ayarlar → Bulut verilerimi sil.
