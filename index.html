Build a cozy island kingdom, one hexagon at a time

TileKingdom is a relaxing tile-laying puzzle game. Place each hex tile where its neighbours love it, merge landmarks, and watch a little kingdom rise out of the sea.

Download on theApp StoreComing soon to iPhone and iPad

A calm puzzle with a lot to discover

Easy to learn, deep to master, and always relaxing.

Smart, satisfying puzzles

Fields love Houses, Rivers love Ponds, Mines hate Forests. Every neighbour changes your score, and three matching tiles merge into a growing landmark.

175 levels, seven worlds

From the Kingdom Lands to the Mushroom Deep, the Ember Core, the Cloud Kingdom, the Red Planet and the Starlight Sea, with a boss level every five stages.

Level up, unlock powers

Everything you do earns XP on the Kingdom Road. Every level brings a gift, and some unlock powers for every game: extra tiles, Undo, Shuffle and Glance.

Many ways to play

Free Play with medals on every island you reach, an Endless kingdom that grows from village to empire, daily tasks, and new rules that arrive one at a time as the story teaches them.

Made to relax

A gentle lo-fi soundtrack where every placement adds a note. No pressure, play at your own pace.

Offline, no account

Play anywhere without a connection. Your progress is backed up to the cloud with a private random ID. English and Turkish.
